# Cirrus Pattern Trainer

A browser flight sim: fly the traffic pattern at Pinecrest (KPCT), a made-up Class D field, in a
Cirrus SR20 or SR22. Talk to Ground and Tower, fly the circuit, land it and taxi back. All of it is
graded. Works on a PC (keyboard, mouse, gamepad) and on a phone (touch; turn it sideways).

**Play:** https://alexschrader.github.io/cirrus-pattern-trainer/

Training toy, not a procedures trainer: speeds and performance are approximations; your POH, your
school's profile and your CFI win every argument.

Release 2.4. Bots fly it through `window.SimBot` (version 1.6).

This repo is published from the sim's one source copy; changes made here are overwritten on the next
publish. 3D model credits: [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).
