This work is based on "Cirrus SR22" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cirrus-sr22-cba602c99c524cd4b40e5c2e5f9c5b4f) by KOG_THORNS (https://sketchfab.com/ioai25312) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes: shown only in outside views (the cockpit is the sim's own), wheels, rudder and elevators
separated to move, the original registration and tail badge removed from the body texture,
repainted at run time in the sim's own paint schemes. Its licence is in `models/sr22/license.txt`.
